About Embrace HD

Embrace HD is a mobile app that prepares your videos for WhatsApp — especially Status — so they stay sharp after upload.

Why we built it

WhatsApp often re-encodes video. A clip that looks fine in your gallery can look soft or blocky once it hits Status. Embrace HD converts to a Status-friendly HD profile, then gets that file back to you on WhatsApp — so you can post or forward without fighting compression yourself.

What the app does

  • Pick a video from your device
  • Convert it with WhatsApp-oriented HD settings (including common Status length options)
  • Deliver the finished video to the WhatsApp number you registered with — ready to save, forward, or post as Status

Accounts & access

You sign in with your WhatsApp number using a one-time password. Trial and subscription options may unlock HD export and control whether ads are shown. Subscription verification can use a third-party billing partner where configured.

Who we are

Embrace HD is operated under the Embrace App brand (embraceapp.co.uk. For product questions or support, use the Contact page.
